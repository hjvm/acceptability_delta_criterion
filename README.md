# meng_project
Research project in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Engineering in Computer Science and Engineering at MIT.

## 2026: the ADC on the Hu, Wilcox et al. (2026) data

[`hu_wilcox_2026/`](hu_wilcox_2026/) applies the delta correlation and the ADC to the LI data released by
Hu, Wilcox, Song, Mahowald & Levy (2026), whose Prediction 2 is the same delta correlation, with their GPT-2 and
Llama-3-70B, BERT, RoBERTa and a Wikipedia 5-gram. See [`hu_wilcox_2026/README.md`](hu_wilcox_2026/README.md).
